Pharmacy Phone Assistant

A phone assistant for chemist shops in Navi Mumbai that takes medicine orders in Hindi, Marathi and English.

नवी मुंबई की केमिस्ट दुकानों के लिए फ़ोन असिस्टेंट।

In development. This service is being built and tested. It is not yet available to shops or customers, and only test data is used.

What it is being built to do

Many chemist shops get calls at all hours. The assistant is designed to answer the shop's phone, talk to callers in their own language, and pass each order to the shop's pharmacist.

  • Answer calls to the shop and take medicine orders in Hindi, Marathi, English, or a mix.
  • Check medicines against the shop's own catalog and stock. It cannot add a medicine that isn't in the catalog.
  • For medicines that need a prescription, send the caller a WhatsApp link to upload a photo of it.
  • Answer simple questions about the shop, such as timings and delivery area.
  • Connect the caller to the pharmacist whenever needed.

Safety first

  • A pharmacist reviews and confirms every order before it is packed.
  • The assistant never approves a prescription. Only the pharmacist can.
  • It does not give medical advice. Those questions go to the pharmacist or a doctor.
  • In an emergency it tells the caller to call 108 or 112 and transfers the call.
  • When asked, it says honestly that it is the shop's digital assistant, not a person.

Messages to customers

When a customer places an order, the shop will send them transactional WhatsApp messages only: a link to upload their prescription, and updates about that order. No promotional messages.

Who it is for

Chemist shops in Navi Mumbai, starting with a small pilot once development is complete.
